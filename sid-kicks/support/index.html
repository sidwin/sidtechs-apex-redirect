Sid Kicks Support

Need help with your shoe collection or wishlist? Contact sid@sidwin.com.

Report a problem

In your email, include:

  • Your Sid Kicks app version and build number.
  • Your device model and iOS or Android version.
  • The steps that led to the problem, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • If available, the error message, with any personal details removed.

You do not need to send your collection, backups, photos, or other private information to ask for help.

Taking photos and scanning labels

In build 14 and later, new and replacement shoe, box-label, and inside-tag photos are taken with the camera. Existing saved photos remain available. The optional guided flow takes you through a shoe photo, box scan, and inside-tag scan, then lets you review and edit the details. You can also enter details manually.

If a scan misses a field, try taking the label photo again in good lighting with the text in focus, or enter that field manually. Scans preserve fields that already contain a value. To correct a value, edit or clear only the intended field. Review suggested details before saving.

If the camera is unavailable, check Sid Kicks' camera permission in your device settings. You can continue entering details manually.

Printed retail and price paid

A reviewed box retail price may fill an empty price-paid field with an unconfirmed amount labeled “Assumed from box retail.” Edit, confirm, or clear it to reflect your purchase. It is not proof of what you paid, does not replace a nonempty price-paid field, and does not set a market appraisal. Appraisals are estimates you enter; Sid Kicks does not provide live valuations.

Protect your collection

Sid Kicks stores your collection locally on your device. There is no Sid Kicks collection server or account, and the app does not sync your collection through a Sid Kicks cloud service.

Save a details-only JSON backup and keep a copy outside the app. Check that the file was actually saved in your chosen location. These JSON backups contain shoe details but exclude all photos. CSV is a spreadsheet export, not a full restorable backup.

Operating-system backups or device transfers are separate. Verify their coverage and successful completion with your device's backup service. Do not assume they include every photo; photo recovery is not guaranteed.

Do not uninstall Sid Kicks or clear its app data to troubleshoot. Doing so can remove your local collection and photos. A details-only JSON backup cannot recover lost photos.

Importing a file

Review the import preview before continuing. Imports add new records rather than overwrite existing pairs, so importing into an existing collection may create duplicates. Existing records and their saved photos are preserved.

A details-only backup adds records without photos. Supported older photo-inclusive backups and selected-pair import files can restore photos included in those files. A selected-pair file clears the sender's paid amounts on import so they are not treated as your purchase prices; restoring your own details backup preserves your saved purchase metadata.

Use the current app version for compatibility. Do not downgrade after saving data with a newer version.
